Study + AI

Free tools that help students in Grades 7–12 study in ways research shows work, with or without AI. Teachers can prepare the material with AI; students do the studying.

Tools

Available now Study Moves Ten study moves for remembering, understanding, solving problems and more. Pick what you want to do and use the move with pen and paper. Teachers can prepare it with AI, and students aged 13 and over can study with AI.
