Settings in Gemini Notebook

  • Language: English, or your class's language
  • Orientation: Landscape
  • Level of detail: Standard
  • Visual style: none of the built-in styles

Age limit

Infographics in Gemini Notebook are only available to users aged 18 and over. Students under 18 must not use the feature themselves, so you make the infographic for the class.

What is an infographics style?

An infographics style is a visual prompt that controls how an AI image generator (or Gemini Notebook, formerly NotebookLM) renders an infographic, slide background, or video-overview frame. This catalogue collects 140 reusable styles — from editorial print to dashboard, retro to neon — each with a ready-to-copy prompt. They work in Gemini Notebook video overviews and slide decks, and also in newer text-aware image models like ChatGPT Images 2 (April 2026) and similar tools.
