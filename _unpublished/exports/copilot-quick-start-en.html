Microsoft Copilot Quick Start · for teachers 01 / 02

The six Copilot features that change classroom work.

Desktop-first, tenant-bounded, takes 10 minutes to learn.

Microsoft Copilot runs inside your school's Microsoft 365 tenant — with commercial data protection on by default when you sign in with a work or school account. These six features give you the strongest daily return. Details on the licensing tiers and the compliance boundary are in Part 1 and Part 3 of the full guide.

Six features, one tenant

1 Copilot Chat

General-purpose chat with tenant-bounded CDP. Ground in your own OneDrive/SharePoint files.

2 Copilot in Word

Draft from a prompt, rewrite selections, summarise documents in seconds.

3 Copilot in Outlook

Summarise long threads, draft replies in tone, coach your writing before sending.

4 Copilot in Teams

Meeting recap with action items. Catch colleagues up in three minutes.

5 Copilot in PowerPoint

Generate slides from a Word doc or outline. Rewrite slide copy on the fly.

6 Copilot Pages

Multiplayer AI-native canvas. Collaborate with colleagues and students in a persistent artifact.

5 things to try in Microsoft 365 today 02 / 02

Try it: 5 practical tasks.

Ten minutes, five moves, one less thing on your plate tonight.

1
Rewrite a parent email in three tonesIn Outlook, open Copilot in compose. Ask for Professional / Friendly / Concise variants. Pick the tone that fits.
2
Summarise a 40-message parent threadOpen the thread in Outlook; tap Summary. Read three bullets in 30 seconds instead of scrolling.
3
Generate a Teams meeting recapEnd your department meeting, open Copilot in the Teams meeting pane, request a recap with action items. Share to the staff wiki.
4
Turn a Word outline into slidesWrite the plan in Word. In PowerPoint, Copilot → Generate from Word. You get a 12-slide starter deck to polish.
5
Draft a differentiation plan in Copilot ChatAsk Copilot Chat, signed into your work account, for three differentiation paths for a mixed-ability Year 8 maths lesson on fractions.
● Watch out · sign-in and data boundary

Sign in with your work or school account every time. Consumer Copilot at copilot.microsoft.com is a different product under different terms — not protected by your school's data agreement. De-identify student data before any prompt. Verify your country's AI-in-education rules — Sweden lacks national guidance as of April 2026.
